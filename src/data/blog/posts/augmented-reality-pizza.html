What is Augmented Reality Pizza?

Augmented reality pizza is an AR experience built to bring digital pizza, menus, games, or other interactive content to the real world via phone, tablet, or screen. Customers can interact with it in a range of ways, from exploring products, ordering, playing games, or bringing a pizza to life for them to order.

AR is different from virtual reality because it doesn't substitute the physical world. It overlays digital content over customers' existing view. 

For instance, a customer may place their cell phone on a table and see an actual pizza appear. They can even flip the pizza, inspect the toppings, alter ingredients, or explore the additional product information

Pizza brands can use augmented reality for:

  • 3D pizza visualization

  • Virtual pizza customization

  • Interactive AR menus

  • Pizza box experiences

  • AR games and promotions

  • Product information

  • Online ordering

This helps bring AR pizza into the entire customer journey, from selecting food to engaging in post-delivery engagement with the brand.

How Does Augmented Reality Pizza Work?

Augmented reality pizza works because the device’s camera can perceive the surroundings and overlay the pizza's content onto the real world, as seen by the end user. This experience could begin via a mobile app, a website, a QR code, or a packaging printout.

A normal AR pizza session looks something like this:

  • Customer unseals the AR experience.

  • They can scan a QR code, open an app, or access a WebAR page.

  • The camera takes pictures of the environment.

  • It can find tables, floors, menus, pizza boxes, and printed pictures.

  • AR software will observe the surface or object.

  • This helps keep the digital content in the right position.

  • A 3D pizza or other digital item is projected.

  • Its screen is displayed on the customer's tablet or smartphone.

  • The customer is engaged in an experience.

  • They may spin a pizza, throw toppings at it, look at the product info, or even play a game.

  • The experience can tie to the next action.

  • This can include opening a menu, using a coupon, or proceeding to order.

It is an AR experience that incorporates app-based AR technology, like ARKit, ARCore, and Unity. Others employ WebAR, which enables customers to open AR with a supported mobile browser without having to download any particular app.

The correct approach will vary depending on the objective. The detailed surface tracking capabilities of a 3D pizza builder may require a different type of AR technology than an AR pizza box game, which could simply use a QR code and image recognition. 

Domino's Pizza Augmented Reality: Build Your Pizza Before Ordering

https://youtu.be/OIP5SajFBDc?si=-u483MA6yL2SEoS4

Domino’s showed how augmented reality pizza could become part of choosing and ordering food with its New Pizza Chef with Augmented Reality.

The feature, which debuted in Australia in 2018, enabled customers to build a virtual pizza via the Domino's current mobile application. In place of looking at a menu to order toppings, they could now see their pizza come into shape in front of them.

Users could choose the preferred toppings and drag them on the virtual pizza:

  • Crust

  • Sauce

  • Cheese

  • Toppings

  • Finishing Swirls

Then the pizza might be viewed from various perspectives in the actual environment of the customers.

To recognize the surrounding environment of the user, Domino's utilized Google ARCore and Apple ARKit. The experience was created with the Unity 3D game engine add-unity-addon-experience.

The company paid close attention to visual accuracy. Domino's said the ingredients were developed to mimic the weights of the ingredients in its pies. The idea was to have the pizza that customers would see in AR closer to the pizza that was presented to them.

The greatest benefit of Domino's Pizza's augmented reality experience was its embedding in the ordering process.

It was not an AR game that lasted a few minutes. Pizza Chef built the app inside the existing Domino's app, enabling the ability to see the menu, offers, and order tracking.

This provided AR with a real job:

Take customers through ground-level illustrations and construction to create the pizza they desired before ordering it.

Domino's eventually summarized their idea as a game where customers can virtually build mixing combinations of pizza ingredients on a table at their place or home. 

The AR Pizza Chef was never a standalone website, but a component of Domino's mobile app.

Pizza Hut's PAC-MAN Augmented Reality Pizza Box

https://euphoriaxr.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Pizza-Hut-WebAR-PAC-MAN®-Box_1080p.mp4

Pizza Hut turned a simple pizza box into a playable PAC-MAN game using WebAR.

The company worked with GSD&M and creative studio Tool to create special-edition pizza boxes. The manual QR code located on the box was scanned with a customer's cell phone. The experience was then opened with a mobile browser.

The customer directed the camera at the top of the pizza box. A PAC-MAN board was created and placed over the object, using image recognition. The surface on which they played was a physical box.

Players could:

  • Control PAC-MAN using an on-screen joystick

  • Choose from 3 difficulty levels

  • Play directly on the pizza box.

  • Enter their initials on a Pizza Hut x PAC-MAN leaderboard

The experience was created with the help of Three.js and A-Frame (3D graphics rendering) as well as 8th Wall (augmented reality). The position of the pizza box was tracked using image recognition, while the customer played the game.

Easy access was one of the greatest benefits. It's constructed with WebAR, making it so that there was no need to install another app for consumers.

The campaign is a great example of how augmented reality Pizza Hut packaging products can bring the experience after delivery. Now that the meal is over, the box doesn't simply disappear, but instead it allows customers to connect with the brand.

How to Create an Augmented Reality Pizza Experience?

The process for the creation of an AR Pizza experience begins with the customer goal and then progresses through experience design, 3D production, development, integration, and testing. 

Define the Goal

Understand how customers can experience what they can do. It could be intended to preview a pizza, add toppings, engage in packing and packaging or play a game, sign up with a promotion or order a pizza.

Choose the AR Experience

Choose the format best suited to the aim. It could be anything from a 3D pizza visualization, a pizza-building AR app, interactive menus, pizza-box experiences, branded games, or any kind of promotional campaign.

Select WebAR or App-Based AR

Determine access to the experience for customers. QR codes can potentially be used with WebAR, packaging can be used, and quick-access campaigns can be successful with WebAR, although app-based AR might be better for deeper functionality or a restaurant app.

Create the 3D Assets

Create digital assets for use in the experience, including pizza bases, crusts, sauces, cheese, toppings, packaging, animations, or branded merchandise. Realistic textures, proportions, and presentation should be used in models of food.

Develop the AR Interactions

Create the functions for customers to utilize, including surface tracking, image recognition, object positioning, pizza rotation, item selection, animation, touch controls, or gamification. 

Connect Business Systems

Integrate the AR experience with relevant systems such as online menus, shopping carts, ordering platforms, coupons, loyalty programs, landing pages, or analytics.

Test Across Devices

An extensive test of it across supported smartphones, operating systems, browsers, lighting, etc., and physical environments is done to provide reliable tracking and a usable experience.

Launch and Measure Performance

Monitor and trace customers' experience after launch. Useful measures can include experience opens, time spent, coupon claims, and visits to the menu, add-to-cart, orders, and repeat engagement.

Technologies Used in Augmented Reality Pizza Experiences

There are various types of augmented reality pizza experiences, depending on the purpose. Some emphasize 3D visualization, some require image tracking, accessing the browser, or interaction with the app.

Common technologies include:

  • WebAR: Use AR through a supported mobile browser, instead of installing an app.

  • ARKit: Apple's AR capability (framework) for building AR experiences on compatible iPhones and iPads.

  • ARCore: Google's platform for AR experiences on supported Android devices.

  • Unity: A game development platform and engine for developing real-time 3D and AR experiences.

  • 8th Wall: A WebAR platform used for browser-based AR campaigns.

  • Vuforia: An AR platform commonly adopted for AR-related image recognition and tracking applications.

  • Three.js: A JavaScript library used to display 3D graphics on the web.

  • A-Frame: A web framework to build 3D and AR scenes.

  • QR codes: Give your customers easy access to an AR experience.

  • Image Recognition: Using the camera to detect a Pizza box, menu, or print piece.

  • 3D modeling: Creates realistic digital pizzas, toppings, packaging, and other assets.

The choice of technology depends on the customer's activities. Picture tracking may be necessary in a pizza-box game, whereas for a 3D pizza viewer, image tracking is not enough, and some surface tracking and realistic 3D models are necessary.

WebAR vs. App-Based AR for Pizza Brands 

WebAR can be implemented as a supported mobile browser with pizza brands or as an app. Usually, WebAR applications are appropriate for short-term engagements such as packaging events and QR-code promotions, whereas app-based AR can prove more useful for a richer set of functionalities and tighter integration with pre-existing ordering and loyalty processes.

Where access friction is the most important factor, WebAR is often more appropriate; when AR should become a more wholly integrated part of an existing ordering or customer application, app-based AR may prove more appropriate.

The price depends on how complex the platform and features are, what integrations are used, 3D assets, and the platform itself.

Yes, WebAR can be used via a mobile browser without an application. 



Can small pizza restaurants use augmented reality?

How long does it take to create an AR pizza experience?

Simple projects may take weeks. Larger applications or games may take longer. 



What devices support augmented reality pizza experiences?

Can augmented reality show the actual size of a pizza?

Yes, but it will depend on the 3D model, the device, the tracking, and the environment.



Can AR pizza experiences connect with online ordering systems?

Yes. They can integrate into menus, carts, coupons, or the checkout. 



Is augmented reality useful for pizza delivery brands?

Yes. AR can be implemented on boxes, receipts, and loyalty materials for delivery brands.



Can augmented reality be added to existing pizza boxes and packaging?

Yes. Packaging can be an AR trigger with QR Codes or image recognition.
